# Music Unbound

**Tired of waiting 20 minutes for a new song to play?**
**Tired of hearing the same handful of tracks because your base is in a plains biome?**
**Ever wondered why Aria Math only plays in Creative?**

Music Unbound gives you control over Minecraft's music without changing how it feels. It still uses the vanilla music system, with the locks taken off.

## Features

- **Every song, everywhere.** Biome locks and Creative-only tracks are gone. Any Overworld song can play anywhere in the Overworld.
- **The newest music on 1.21.1.** The tracks from Chase the Skies and Chaos Cubed (Below and Above, Broken Clocks, Fireflies, Lilypad, O's Piano, Ebb, Home, Memories, Nightly, Shores) play in 1.21.1.
- **Playlists.** Pick a built-in playlist, play the game as it sounded in an older version, or build your own. Mark favourites to hear them more often.
- **Your timing.** Default, Frequent, Constant, Era-accurate, or your own minimum and maximum wait between songs.
- **A music player in the pause menu.** See what's playing and how far along it is. Skip, go back, or pause.
- **Play now.** Pick any song from the track list and it starts immediately.
- **Dedicated music where it matters.** The Nether, the End, the Deep Dark and underwater can keep their own music, or share your playlist.
- **Small touches.**
  - A Now Playing toast
  - Music that keeps playing while the game is paused
  - Music that fades down near a jukebox
  - Smooth fades between songs
  - A new song when you change dimension, at sunrise or nightfall, or when a thunderstorm starts
  - Keybinds for next, previous and pause

Music discs are never added to the rotation.

Set the frequency to **Era-accurate** to also get that version's real wait between songs. These timings were read from the original game code. In 1.7, Creative music played every 1 to 3 minutes. In Alpha, the first song came 0 to 10 minutes after joining.

## Where to find it

*Options > Music & Sounds > Music Unbound...*, or the Config button in the mod list.

## Client-side

Install it on your client only. It works on any server, vanilla or modded, and the server doesn't need it.

## About the downloads

Minecraft's music can't be redistributed inside a mod, so Music Unbound **contains no audio**. On first launch it downloads the newer tracks (about 33 MB) once from **Mojang's official asset servers**, the same place the launcher downloads from. They go into the launcher's shared assets folder and are checked for integrity. If you have already played 1.21.6 or newer, nothing is downloaded. After that, everything works offline. You can turn downloads off in the settings.

New background tracks Mojang adds in future versions are picked up automatically. No mod update is needed.

## FAQ

**Does it work with biome mods?**
Yes. Music from other mods is listed under "Other mods" and can be added to your playlists.

**Does it change the music files?**
No. It decides which vanilla track plays and when. Resource packs that replace music still work.

**Can I go back to vanilla?**
Yes. Pick the *Vanilla 1.21.1* playlist and the mod stops changing the music.
